  • Our Story

From a Small Workshop to a Trusted Motor Partner

Founded in 1998 in the industrial city of Coimbatore, KMP Industries began with a simple vision: to manufacture reliable, efficient water pumps that would serve India's growing water management needs. What started as a small-scale operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted pump manufacturers. Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, unwavering commitment to quality, and deep understanding of our customers' needs. From agricultural farmers seeking efficient irrigation solutions to industrial enterprises requiring robust water management systems, we've consistently delivered products that exceed expectations. Today, we operate a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility equipped with advanced technology and employ skilled professionals who share our commitment to excellence. Our products are trusted by over 50,000 customers across India and beyond.
From your first consultation to final installation and long-term support, we focus on clear guidance, quality workmanship, and solar systems built to deliver real performance for years.
